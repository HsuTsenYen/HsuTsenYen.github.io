I want to understand the social and relational drivers of human motivation within organizations: how everyday relationships between colleagues shape whether people speak up, support one another, and engage at work.

## Working Paper

**Social Exchange and Rational Assessment: How Affective and Cognitive Trust Promote Public Voice Support**  
**H.-T. Yen**, T.-Y. Wu, Y.-H. Wen, & Y.-Y. Liu (in progress)  
*Presented at the 2026 Taiwan Association of Industrial and Organizational Psychology Annual Conference and International Symposium.*

Most research on employee voice looks at the person speaking up. This project looks at the colleagues who hear them, and asks when those colleagues will publicly endorse a coworker's voice. By bringing in everyday relational variables, we find that affective and cognitive trust both foster public voice endorsement, through two parallel mechanisms: **social exchange** and **rational assessment of efficacy**.
